Co-op crew

Climb together. Everyone opens this same page and enters the same crew code. Enemies get tougher with each extra player, and downed players get back up after 10 seconds.

Smash-style free-for-all on a floating stage. Nobody loses health: every hit raises your damage %, and the higher it gets, the further hits launch you. Knock your friends off the edge to score a K.O. Everyone opens this page and enters the same arena code.
